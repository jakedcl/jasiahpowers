# Walking Jasiah through the site

Open [jasiahpowers.com/studio](https://www.jasiahpowers.com/studio) on a computer. He signs in with the Sanity account that already has access.

1. **Homepage.** Click Homepage. Featured work is the row of projects on the front page. Add a project, then drag the rows into the order he wants. That order is only the homepage. The video at the bottom is Featured YouTube video: paste a YouTube link. Click Publish when it looks right.

2. **Add or edit a project.** Click Projects, then the project, or create a new one. Project name is required. Short summary is the one line under the picture. Project text is the longer writeup: headings, bold, italic, links, and bullet lists. To put a picture in the text, insert a picture, upload it, and add a caption if he wants one. To put a YouTube video in the text, insert YouTube and paste the link. Project images are the pictures at the bottom of the page. Click Generate on the web address after the name.

3. **Reorder projects.** On the Projects list, drag a project up or down. That order is the Projects page. It does not change Featured work.

4. **Photos.** Click Photos. Add a photo, then drag the photos into order. Every photo needs alt text: a few plain words describing it. That is what a screen reader reads, and it shows if the picture is slow. A caption is optional and shows when someone taps the photo. Click Publish.

5. **YouTube playlist.** Click Site settings. In YouTube playlist, paste the playlist link from YouTube. Click Publish. To hide a video on the site, make that video private or delete it on YouTube. The site skips private and deleted videos. He does not have to take them out of the playlist.

6. **Social links and the booking email.** Still in Site settings. Fill in only the social links he wants. An empty link stays off the site. Music link is the Music item in the header. Contact email is where booking requests are sent. Booking types are the choices on the contact form. Click Publish.

7. **What Publish means.** A change stays a draft until he clicks Publish. After Publish, the live site catches up in about a minute.

8. **Where booking emails go.** The contact form emails the Contact email in Site settings. Right now that is jasiahsteez@gmail.com. If that field is empty, the email goes to the backup address on the server, which is the same inbox.
